What The Numbers Say

The fine-tuned model lost: it learned to reproduce IGDB's lists, noise included, and got worse on the golden set. A simple floor of 10 ratings won, because it removes fan projects and obscure games that sit close to famous ones.

The golden set has 30 games with 5 expected results each. It was drafted with an AI assistant before any result was seen, so it's strict and small: a good game that isn't on the list counts as wrong. Part of the floor's gain comes from that, since unknown games rarely make such a list.
